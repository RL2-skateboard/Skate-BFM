# Stage 06

Status: WAITING_REVIEW. MuJoCo is the active complete backend; Isaac is excluded.

Implemented first rider slice:

- official G1 29DoF MJCF and procedural standard board in one compiled scene;
- `none`, `pose`, and real BFM `policy` controller choices;
- `separate`, `push_start`, and `steer_start` presets;
- board/robot naming remains separate; root state uses pelvis/deck names;
- `separate`: pelvis is 1 m along world -Y from deck center, board and robot are stationary, and no foot/deck contact exists;
- `push_start`: right ankle-roll foot proxy is initialized over the deck and left foot proxy at ground level, verified from compiled contact pairs;
- `steer_start`: both ankle-roll foot proxies are initialized over the deck and the board free joint has +X velocity 1 m/s;
- records include controller, preset, BFM task/checkpoint context, state and
  diagnostics. A free-base fall is recorded as policy behavior, not hidden.

Commands:

```bash
python play.py --backend mujoco --scene rider --controller none --preset separate --duration 5 --record runs/06_separate
python play.py --backend mujoco --scene rider --controller pose --preset push_start --duration 10 --record runs/06_push_start
python play.py --backend mujoco --scene rider --controller pose --preset steer_start --duration 10 --record runs/06_steer_start
python play.py --backend mujoco --scene rider --controller policy --policy-task reward --checkpoint checkpoints/bfm-zero --preset steer_start --duration 30 --record runs/06_policy
python play.py --replay runs/06_policy --mode visual
```

The policy checkpoint is a public BFM ground policy context, not a task-trained
skateboard policy. Stable riding is not claimed.

The compiled initialization prints pelvis/deck/foot positions, quaternion,
velocities, contact pairs, penetration and termination state. No support bar,
support spring, equality weld or vertical stabilization force is generated.
Stage 06 is not ACCEPTED until the window review is complete.

Foot entity audit: `left_ankle_roll_link` and `right_ankle_roll_link` are the
foot rigid-body ancestors; their `dummy_lf_*` and `dummy_rf_*` descendants are
the finite collision proxies used for contact classification. The ankle-pitch
links are not relabeled as sole bodies.

Compiled initial-state evidence from the current MuJoCo model:

```text
separate:   pelvis/deck horizontal=1.000 m, feet/deck=0, board v=(0,0,0)
push_start: left/ground=2, right/deck=1, right/ground=0, board v=(0,0,0)
steer_start:left/deck=2, right/deck=2, board v=(1,0,0) m/s
```

The current official foot proxy and procedural deck overlap by about 11–12 mm
in push/steer initialization. This is recorded in `penetration_min_m` and keeps
stage 06 at `WAITING_REVIEW`; it must be reduced to the requested millimetre
range before marking the stage accepted.

Parameter sources: G1 geometry/joints come from the vendored BFM-Zero deploy
MJCF; pose/default/action-scale/KP/KD come from
`configs/bfm/motivo_newG1.yaml`; board dimensions/masses/truck values are the
procedural engineering assumptions in `models/board.py`; the three preset root
placements and initial speed are explicit Stage-06 experiment conditions.
